# Text Classification Annotation Guidelines

## Objective
Classify text into four categories based on its meaning and intent.

## Categories

### 1. Positive
Text expressing satisfaction, happiness, or appreciation.

### 2. Negative
Text expressing dissatisfaction, disappointment, or an unfavorable opinion.

### 3. Neutral
Text providing factual information without expressing a positive or negative opinion.

### 4. Complaint
Text reporting a problem, service failure, or requesting resolution.

## Annotation Rules
- Read the entire sentence before labeling.
- Focus on the actual meaning.
- Assign one primary label per sentence.
- Do not guess missing information.
- Apply labels consistently.

## Quality Standards
- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Clear labeling
- Correct interpretation
